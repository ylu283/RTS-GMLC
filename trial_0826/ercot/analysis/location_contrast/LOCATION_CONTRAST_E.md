## 7. What the reads say (interpretation; the verdicts above stand as computed)

- **The rule reads LOCATION EFFECT for V1 on all four pairs.** At matched total, the bus-120 rows relieve more curtailment per unit of H_C than the bus-2/75 rows (e 0.820 vs 0.804 at 800 MW; 0.905 vs 0.840 in the matched-u pair). H_C scaling does not remove the difference.
- **This is not evidence of a NETWORK effect.** All six sites share one PTDF on L252 and one LMP. H_C counts every curtailed hour equally, but relief in an hour is also bounded by how much is curtailed in that hour. A site whose output is high in the deep-curtailment hours relieves more per MWh of H_C: that is a wind-profile effect. §3 predicted in advance a profile-only difference of ≈ 9 rulers for the matched-u pair; the observed residual is 6.7 rulers. The covariate H_C was too coarse to absorb the profile. The pre-registered label is kept; it means "not explained by H_C", not "network".
- **Size non-linearity also enters the pairs against row 6.** e falls with size on the same composition (P1: 0.816 → 0.797 → 0.775; row 6 → row 9: 0.787 → 0.730). The linear H_C scaling ignores this fall.
- N4: pair 4–5 is COMPOSITION, and the other three pairs read LOCATION EFFECT with the same caveat. N1 is not readable on any pair, as expected (chatter-limited).
- E1 / E3: every location contrast is NOT READABLE or WITHIN GAP BOUND. No cost statement can be made.
