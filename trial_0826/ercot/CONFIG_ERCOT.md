### RUC termination riders (non-negotiable, Kay 09-19)

1. **Per-day solution-quality logging in every run**: final MIPGap,
   termination status, SolCount per RUC day → committed
   `extracts/<run>/ruc_quality.csv`, parsed from the Gurobi logs
   (`output_solver_logs=True` on ALL runs).
2. **Determinism caveat (prominent)**: a time-limited MILP returns
   machine-load-dependent incumbents. The RTS bit-reproducibility
   convention does NOT carry to ERCOT. The noise model is re-established
   empirically by the screening wave's 2 replicate rows (one exact base
   repeat, one duplicated screening point); **any screening-delta claim
   requires |Δ| ≫ replicate spread — deltas inside the spread are
   "within solver noise", never effects.**
3. If smoke/probes show worst-day gaps ≳ 10%, REPORT to Kay with the
   numbers before the base submit (decision stands unless he reverses).
4. **SolCount=0 rider** (worse than a large gap): median uncapped solve is
   730 s, so a hard day can hit 120 s with NO incumbent — Prescient then
   dies mid-year. If any smoke/probe day shows SolCount=0, add
   incumbent-finding options (`MIPFocus=1`, optionally `NoRelHeurTime`)
   via `--ruc_solver_option` or raise TimeLimit BEFORE the base submit.
   In the full-year quality log, SolCount=0 on any day is
   **run-invalidating — reported, never averaged over.**

Screening budget note: the RTS retrofit-speedup ratio (2.7×) does NOT
transfer under a time cap — truncated days cost 120 s regardless of
instance ease, so every run has a ~12 h floor. Screening ≈ N × 15–22 h
wall (parallel, no `-tc` cap), not N × 15/2.7.

## Hydro decision (T1.2 — decided, FLAGGED to Kay/PI)

TX-123BT provides NO hydro timeseries, and HYDRO is a renewable type: with
no pointers the 10 units (497.8 MW total) have scalar PMax = constant
availability — free flat "baseload". GTEP dropped hydro entirely.
**Decision: KEEP the units with the constant-PMax caveat** (matches the
vendored gen.csv; 497.8 MW ≈ 0.9% of the 53 GW peak).
**Regardless of keep/drop: `Unit Type == HYDRO` is excluded from ALL
curtailment rankings, curtailment objectives, and anatomy curtailment
figures** — `get_renewables_curtailment()` computes `p_max − pg` over
every nondispatchable, so every hour hydro backs down would register as
fake "renewables curtailment" (inflating g2's analogue and corrupting the
top-curtailers screening seed). `unit_type` is a column in every
per-generator extract, making the filter trivial.

## DA ≡ RT (documented consequence — repeat wherever shed is discussed)

All three DAY_AHEAD/REAL_TIME profile pairs are byte-identical (equal
MD5s in `MD5SUMS.vendored`): there is zero forecast error. Unserved
energy reflects genuine capacity/transmission scarcity only; the RTS
conservative-DA mechanism (commitment set against forecasts the RT stage
corrects) **cannot operate the same way here**. Every cross-system shed
comparison carries this footer.
