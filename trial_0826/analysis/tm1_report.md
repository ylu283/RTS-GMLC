## Task 4 — ω bound recommendations

curtailment-P95 ratio, capped at 1.0, then rounded **up** to one decimal.

**Wind hitting the 1.0 cap is the expected outcome, not a bug**: in
cheap-LMP hours wind availability sits near nameplate (raw ratios
0.98), so availability — not curtailment — is the binding notion of
harvestable energy, exactly as doc 14 §3.1 argues. PV lands at 0.8 as
anticipated (night zeros occupy ~30–43% of cheap hours but P90 tolerates
them; daylight-only P90s are only ~1–4 MW higher).

## Summary

1. **Wind ω bounds widen**: doc 14 §3.1's default wind box [0.05, 0.5] is
   too narrow — all four wind sites (303, 317, 122, 309) recommend
   **[0.05, 1.0]** because P90 available-at-cheap-LMP ≈ 0.98 × nameplate.
2. **PV ω bounds widen**: the [0.02, 0.3] placeholder is far below the data —
   all six PV sites recommend **[0.02, 0.8]** (P90 avail | LMP < 15 ratios
   0.76–0.80, all rounding up to 0.8).
3. The curtailment-P95 floor never binds: availability dominates at every
   site (most dramatically 309_WIND_1: curt-P95 ω = 0.01 vs avail ω = 0.98).
4. OAT reference points for the T-M2 screening batch (doc 14 §6, item 2):
   **ω = 0.5 for wind, ω = 0.4 for PV** (per-site values in
   `tm1_per_site_stats.csv`, `omega_oat_reference`).
5. All curtailment-hours (> 5 MW) coincide with LMP ≤ 1 at every site, and
   cheap-hour fractions are similar across buses (27–37% below 15 $/MWh) —
   the LMP < 15 criterion is not sensitive to bus choice within the top-10.
6. Sanity gate: 8784 h × 80 generators, zero NaNs, system and 303 totals
   match doc 14 §1 exactly; 314_PV_3 ≈ 22.4 GWh confirmed (informational
   only, not a campaign site).
